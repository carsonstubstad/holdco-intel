## 1. Principles
- Answer, per company: who is growing and is it speeding up, what margin it makes, what it guides to, how the market took its last results, when it reports next, and how big it is (v3.0). The overview opens with The read, then answers one question at a time for all six: the ranked rows under the switch (Growth, Margin, Year to date, Size), each row with its period, its change against its base and its next results date; then share price beside the "Results and the market" map, which shows growth and the market's reaction at once. A company page answers all of them for one company, and Compare puts two to six side by side, metrics as rows. (v2.0's peer table answered all five in one sweep; it goes in 26b.)
- Three views, chosen by link (v3.0). The overview always shows all six; the masthead's tab row reaches every view from every view; every company name, ranked row, bubble and strip dot opens that company's page; Compare shows the companies its hash names. The URL hash is the only state, with no browser storage, and v2.0 links still open (section 6). The chart key is a key, not a control. Each view prints itself (X1, X2). (v2.0's capsule, the one place to pick companies, goes in 26b.)
- Three voices. What we compute or label is set in the system sans; management's verbatim words in the system serif; the Claude-drafted Industry read in the sans, verbatim, never cut, always with its label saying who drafted it, that a script checked every figure, that the maintainer approved it, and its date (T2). The lines computed beside it are labelled as computed. Quotes are never cut short on screen. Guidance values are data and may clamp, with the full text one hover or one view away.
- Numbers lead. Tabular figures, the true minus, a sign on every change, each figure as reported with its period, and the period one click from its source. Every change names what it is compared against ("vs Q2 26"); every dash says why. A converted or estimated figure carries "≈" (market cap, and revenue converted to USD).
- Color has one job each. Company colors are identity only: the chart's lines, key swatches, end dots and leaders, and from v3.0 a dot before a company's name (the ranked rows, the company page, Compare's heads). Never text, never a fill behind text, never direction. Green and red mean direction and always come with ▲ or ▼ and a sign: every signed move in a table cell or a list row takes them, the share moves too (J2); a figure inside a sentence, such as a computed line, stays ink. Coral is the brand mark only. Nothing relies on color alone.
- Spend boldness once: the ink masthead (T1, from v3.0; it was the dark capsule). Everything else is quiet: no shadow, no gradient, no hover lift. A panel's top holds only its title and its own controls (L1).
- Every panel falls back to one grey sentence, and every view prints as a meeting handout whose page 1 stands alone.

## 9. Out of scope
- **Fonts from a CDN or self-hosted.** The system stacks only; DM Sans was tried in round 4 and declined (2026-10-26).
- **An "All themes" tab.** Decided in v2.0 planning. (Market cap, 8-quarter bars and Claude-drafted text, out in v2.0, came in with v3.0: V3, V4 and T2's labelled read.)
- **A peer median row.** The six growth figures cover different periods (Q3, Q2, H1).
- **Remembering the view in the browser.** It lives in the URL hash only.
- **D3 or any second chart library.** No new dependency; small pictures are inline SVG built in the DOM (U1, V4). (v3.0 draws no sparklines: one bar per ranked row, U1.)
- **Real company logos.** A dot and the name instead (v3.0).
- **A 3D chart or a 3D industry map.** The CSP has no `'unsafe-eval'`, so no WebGL library and no Plotly 3D traces; a rotating chart takes a phone swipe. The one 3D element is T4's maker's mark, CSS transforms only; the one map is U4's, flat inline SVG.
- **A clickable share chart, or one that highlights a line from a row.** Tried in step 25's round 3: under `hovermode: 'x unified'` a tap returns every line at that date, so it could open the wrong company; the rows, tabs and map already open every company.
- **News.** Parked in PLAN.md with the 2026-10-29 probe.
- **An activity timeline.** Designed in step 25's round 1 and dropped by the maintainer: The read covers the latest round, the rows carry each next results date, a company page lists its releases.
- **An Industry read that updates itself.** Hard rule 2: the read is drafted only at `/curate` on the maintainer's laptop, checked by a script and approved.
- **Tailwind, a framework or a build step.**
- **Rich text in quotes or hover labels.** Text goes in via `textContent`; hover text is escaped.
- **Sorting the unified hover by value.** Plotly 2.35.2 has no option for it.
- **Sorting Compare by a row, highlighting a line from the key, dark mode.** In the Backlog (the ranked rows sort the overview).
